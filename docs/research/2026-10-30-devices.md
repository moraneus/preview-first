## Frame assets

No permissive library ships accurate 2025-26 frames. Draw frames procedurally in CSS from viewport + corner radius + cutout. Structural reference: https://github.com/jhildenbiddle/css-device-frames (MIT). Data: https://github.com/kylebshr/ScreenCorners (MIT), Chrome EmulatedDevices.ts (BSD-3). Do not bundle Meta Devices or Apple Design Resources bezels (licences forbid redistribution).
